🧲 Magnetising a Nail

The Stroking Method

Holding the magnet upright, drag its tip from the head to the tip of the nail, then let go to lift it away. Always stroke in the same direction — switching direction will make the nail lose its magnetism! Then try leaving it alone for a while and see what happens.
